1. Introduction to Heart Disease Prediction
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Heart disease is one of the major health challenges worldwide. It refers to a range of conditions that affect the heart and its ability to function properly. Common risk factors include high blood pressure, high cholesterol, diabetes, smoking, obesity, lack of physical activity, and age. Early identification of heart disease risk can help healthcare professionals take preventive measures and support better patient care.

Traditional methods of heart disease diagnosis involve medical history, physical examinations, laboratory tests, and other clinical investigations. However, analyzing multiple health-related factors can be complex and time-consuming. Machine Learning (ML) provides an opportunity to analyze medical data, identify patterns, and predict the likelihood of heart disease.

This project focuses on developing a Machine Learning-based Heart Disease Prediction System that uses patient health information to predict whether a person may have heart disease. The project explores different classification algorithms, such as Logistic Regression, K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN), Decision Tree, Random Forest, Support Vector Machine (SVM), and XGBoost, to compare their performance and identify a suitable model.

The system uses features such as age, sex, chest pain type, resting blood pressure, cholesterol level, maximum heart rate, and other clinical measurements, depending on the dataset. Data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, model training, and evaluation are performed to understand the data and assess the predictive performance of the models.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Objectives of the Project
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Analyze the heart disease dataset and identify important health-related features.

* Perform data cleaning, preprocessing, and exploratory data analysis.

* Train multiple Machine Learning classification algorithms.

* Compare models using accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and a confusion matrix.

* Select a suitable model based on evaluation results.

* Develop a system that can estimate heart disease risk from the provided input data.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Conclusion
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Heart Disease Prediction project demonstrates how Machine Learning can be applied to healthcare data to support risk assessment and decision-making. It may help identify patterns associated with heart disease and support further medical evaluation. However, the model is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis and should not be used alone to make treatment decisions. Its reliability depends on the quality of the dataset, model validation, and testing on representative patient populations.
